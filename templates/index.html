SECURITY & PRIVACY

Care information deserves careful handling.

MedTrack separates patient and doctor access, protects sign-in with hashed passwords, and keeps AWS permissions separate from application accounts. This college project is a coordination demo, not an emergency or clinical decision service.

Role-aware accessEach portal is protected by server-side authorization.
Clear data boundariesLocal SQLite and an optional AWS DynamoDB repository share a service contract.
Cloud-ready foundationPrepared for a future EC2, IAM, DynamoDB, and optional SNS deployment.
